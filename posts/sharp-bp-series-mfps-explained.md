# Sharp BP Series MFPs Explained: Which Model Is Right for Your Business?

Choosing an office printer can feel confusing. The Sharp BP Series alone includes compact colour machines for small offices, faster models for busy departments, high-volume systems for central print rooms, and black-and-white machines for businesses that mainly print text.

The right choice is rarely the fastest model on the list. The best machine is the one that matches how your office actually works: how much you print, whether you need colour, how much you scan, how much space you have, and how sensitive your documents are.

This guide explains each group in the BP Series in plain terms and shows which type of business it suits best. It covers these models only:

- **Colour:** BP-22C20, BP-22C25, BP-30C25, BP-51C26, BP-51C31, BP-51C36, BP-51C45, BP-51C55, BP-61C31, BP-61C36, BP-71C45, BP-71C55, BP-71C65
- **Black and white:** BP-22M24L, BP-22M22, BP-22M24, BP-22M28, BP-22M31, BP-51M31, BP-51M36, BP-51M45, BP-51M55, BP-51M65, BP-71M55, BP-71M65

Specifications and options can vary by market and configuration, so always confirm the final details with an authorised Sharp dealer.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - All BP Series models are A3 multifunction printers (MFPs): they print, copy, and scan, and handle paper up to A3 size, twice the size of a standard A4 sheet.
> - In most model names, the last two digits show the A4 print speed in pages per minute. A BP-51C36, for example, prints up to 36 colour pages per minute.
> - The first number shows the group: 22 and 30 are compact, 51 is the all-round departmental range, 61 adds advanced cloud and sign-in features, and 71 is the high-performance range.
> - "C" means colour and "M" means monochrome (black and white only).
> - Choose based on real monthly volume, colour needs, scanning workload, and security, not just top speed.

## How to Read a Sharp BP Model Name

Once you know the pattern, Sharp's model names tell you most of what you need. Take the **BP-51C36** as an example:

- **BP** is the product family.
- **51** is the series. Higher numbers generally mean more capable, faster machines with more features.
- **C** means colour. An **M** (as in BP-51M36) means monochrome, which prints in black and white only.
- **36** is the A4 print speed: up to 36 pages per minute (ppm).

A few other terms appear throughout this guide:

- **A3 and A4:** A4 is the standard office sheet. A3 is double that size, used for plans, posters, and large spreadsheets. All BP models can print A3, but more slowly than A4.
- **Images per minute (ipm):** how fast the machine scans. One two-sided page counts as two images.
- **Document feeder:** the tray on top that pulls in a stack of originals for scanning or copying. A **single-pass** feeder reads both sides of a page at once, which is much faster than a **reversing** feeder that flips each page over.

## Start With Your Real Requirements

Before comparing models, look at how your office actually uses documents. Six questions will narrow the choice quickly.

### 1. Do you need colour?

Choose colour if you regularly produce presentations, marketing material, diagrams, plans, or customer-facing documents where colour improves clarity. Choose black and white if most of your output is contracts, invoices, reports, delivery notes, and other text-based records. If colour is rarely needed, a monochrome machine keeps things simpler.

### 2. How much do you print?

Speed (ppm) matters, but it is only part of the picture. Also consider:

- How many people will share the machine
- Whether several teams print at the same time
- Whether long jobs, such as 200-page reports, are common
- Whether print volume is growing

A small office printing a few hundred pages a month has very different needs from a legal team producing large document bundles every day.

### 3. How much do you scan?

Scanning is often overlooked. A business that scans invoices, contracts, or patient files in large batches may need a fast single-pass feeder even if it prints relatively little.

### 4. How much paper should it hold?

Low-volume offices can manage with standard trays. Busy departments should add extra trays so staff are not constantly refilling paper.

### 5. Do you need finishing?

Finishing means what happens after printing: stapling, hole punching, folding, or making booklets. These are useful for reports, training packs, and customer documents, but many offices never need them.

### 6. What security do you need?

A networked MFP handles confidential information. Consider user sign-in, secure print release (documents only print when the right person is at the machine), encryption, data erasure, audit logs, and malware protection. Involve your IT team before you decide.

## BP-22C Series: Compact Entry-Level Colour

The **BP-22C20** and **BP-22C25** are compact colour MFPs for small offices that need A3 colour without a large departmental machine.

According to Sharp's specifications, the BP-22C25 prints up to 25 pages per minute on A4 (14 ppm on A3), and the BP-22C20 prints up to 20 ppm on A4 (12 ppm on A3). Both hold 350 sheets as standard, expandable to 1,850 sheets with extra trays, and use a 100-sheet reversing feeder that scans at up to 31 ipm ([Sharp](https://global.sharp/products/copier/products/bp_22c25/specifications/index.html)).

**Best suited to:** small offices, start-ups, reception areas, and professional practices with moderate colour printing and limited floor space.

**Which one?** Choose the BP-22C25 if several people share the machine or you expect printing to grow. The BP-22C20 suits lighter, occasional use. Neither is the right choice for a busy department with heavy batch scanning.

## BP-30C25: Flexible Colour for Connected Teams

The **BP-30C25** is a 25 ppm colour MFP for small and medium offices that want more flexibility than the entry-level models.

It holds 350 sheets as standard (expandable to 1,850), handles thicker paper up to 256 gsm (thick card stock), and its 100-sheet feeder scans in colour at up to 52 ipm, much faster than the BP-22C models ([Sharp](https://www.sharp.eu/sharp-bp-30c25)).

A useful feature is print release across connected machines. A staff member sends a document to print, then collects it from any authorised BP device by signing in. Confidential pages are not left waiting in a tray, and staff can collect prints on whichever floor they happen to be.

**Best suited to:** small and medium offices, teams spread across floors or departments, and offices that need stronger scanning than a basic compact model.

## BP-51C Series: Balanced Departmental Colour

The **BP-51C** range is the all-round workhorse of the BP colour line, and for many businesses it is the most sensible choice. It covers five speeds, and all models use a 100-sheet single-pass feeder that scans at up to 80 ipm ([Sharp](https://www.sharp.eu/printers-photocopiers/explore-sharp-photocopiers/a3-colour-photocopiers)).

| Model | A4 speed | Best for |
|---|---:|---|
| BP-51C26 | 26 ppm | Small department with regular colour printing |
| BP-51C31 | 31 ppm | Medium office with shared daily use |
| BP-51C36 | 36 ppm | Busy department with frequent printing and scanning |
| BP-51C45 | 45 ppm | Larger department with heavy document traffic |
| BP-51C55 | 55 ppm | High-demand office or several teams sharing one machine |

**Best suited to:** medium-sized businesses, professional services firms, schools and training centres, and offices producing customer-facing colour documents.

**Which one?** Choose the BP-51C26 or BP-51C31 for moderate use. Move up to the BP-51C36, BP-51C45, or BP-51C55 when several teams share the machine or long print jobs are common.

## BP-61C Series: Advanced Colour With Cloud Features

The **BP-61C31** and **BP-61C36** are colour MFPs for businesses where the printer must fit into a wider digital, cloud-based workplace.

The BP-61C31 prints up to 31 ppm in colour and black and white, and its 100-sheet single-pass feeder scans at up to 80 ipm. What sets this range apart is its built-in connection to Microsoft services: a Microsoft Teams connector lets staff print from and scan straight into Teams channels at the machine, and support for Microsoft Entra ID lets staff sign in with their normal work account ([Sharp](https://www.sharp.eu/sharp-bp-61c31)). The BP-61C36 offers the same approach at 36 ppm for busier departments ([Sharp](https://www.sharp.eu/sharp-bp-61c36)).

**Best suited to:** medium and large businesses using Microsoft 365, organisations that need tight control over who uses the printer, and departments handling confidential documents.

## BP-71C Series: High-Performance Colour

The **BP-71C45**, **BP-71C55**, and **BP-71C65** are built for heavy colour workloads where many users send large jobs throughout the day.

The BP-71C55 prints up to 55 ppm on A4 and 28 ppm on A3 ([Sharp](https://www.sharp.eu/sharp-bp-71c55)). The BP-71C65 raises that to 65 ppm on A4 and 33 ppm on A3 ([Sharp](https://www.sharp.eu/sharp-bp-71c65)). The BP-71C45, at 45 ppm, is the entry point to the range.

**Best suited to:** large corporate departments, central print rooms, universities, government offices, and businesses that cannot afford staff waiting at the printer.

**Before you choose:** these are large machines. Check floor space, power supply, finishing needs, and service support in your location.

## BP-22M Series: Compact Black and White

The **BP-22M22**, **BP-22M24**, **BP-22M24L**, **BP-22M28**, and **BP-22M31** are compact monochrome MFPs for offices that mainly print text: invoices, contracts, forms, delivery notes, and internal reports.

The lower-speed models suit small offices, while the BP-22M28 and BP-22M31 give busier teams more capacity. The BP-22M24L is a variant of the 24 ppm model; ask your dealer how its configuration differs. Sharp does not publish identical specifications for every market, so confirm exact speeds and configurations for this group with your dealer.

**Best suited to:** small businesses, administrative and back-office teams, and reception areas where colour is occasional rather than essential.

## BP-51M Series: Departmental Black and White

The **BP-51M31**, **BP-51M36**, **BP-51M45**, **BP-51M55**, and **BP-51M65** cover speeds from 31 to 65 ppm for steadily heavier black-and-white workloads. They print, copy, scan, fax, and store documents. The BP-51M45, for example, prints at 45 ppm and scans at up to 80 ipm through a 100-sheet single-pass feeder ([Sharp](https://www.sharp.eu/sharp-bp-51m45)).

**Which one?** The BP-51M31 and BP-51M36 suit medium offices. The BP-51M45, BP-51M55, and BP-51M65 suit busy departments where many people print at once.

**Best suited to:** legal and finance teams, logistics offices, schools and colleges, and any business that rarely needs colour but prints a lot.

## BP-71M Series: High-Volume Black and White

The **BP-71M55** and **BP-71M65** are the fastest black-and-white machines in this guide, built to act as a central document hub.

The BP-71M55 prints up to 55 ppm. Its standout feature is the scanner: a 300-sheet feeder scans both sides of each page in one pass, at up to 300 images per minute. That means a 150-page double-sided contract can be scanned in about a minute. Like the BP-61C range, it supports Microsoft Entra ID sign-in and includes a Microsoft Teams connector ([Sharp](https://www.sharp.eu/sharp-bp-71m55)). The BP-71M65 raises print speed to 65 ppm for even heavier output.

**Best suited to:** large administrative departments, central document-processing teams, public-sector offices, and businesses that scan large volumes daily. These machines are unnecessary for a small office, but valuable where slow printing or scanning would hold up many people.

## Final Checklist Before You Order

Before choosing, confirm:

- Average and peak monthly print volume
- Rough split between colour and black-and-white pages
- Number of users and departments sharing the machine
- A3 and special paper needs (thick card, labels, envelopes)
- Daily scanning volume and feeder speed required
- Paper capacity and extra trays
- Finishing options such as stapling or booklets
- User sign-in and secure print release
- Cloud and Microsoft 365 integration
- Available floor space and power supply
- Installation, service, and support arrangements
- Expected growth over the next few years

The best BP Series MFP is not the fastest or most advanced model. It is the one that matches your real workload without paying for capacity you will never use. A small office may be perfectly served by the BP-22 range, while a busy department may need the speed and workflow features of the BP-51, BP-61, or BP-71 Series. A careful assessment now means a printer that supports productivity and security for its whole working life.
