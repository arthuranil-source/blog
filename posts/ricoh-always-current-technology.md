# Ricoh Always Current Technology: What It Is and Why It Matters for Your Business

Think about the phone in your pocket. You bought it once, but it keeps getting better: new features appear and security holes get fixed. Office printers traditionally did not work like that. The machine you installed on day one stayed exactly the same until the day you replaced it.

Ricoh Always Current Technology changes that. This guide explains in plain terms what it is, how it works, what it actually delivers, and whether it matters for your business.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - Ricoh Always Current Technology is a built-in update system on Ricoh's newer "Intelligent Devices" (the IM series of multifunction printers), introduced in early 2019.
> - It lets your printer download new features, security improvements, and apps during its life, without waiting for a new machine.
> - The main business benefits are better security, a longer useful life for the device, less downtime, and more value from the same investment.
> - It is only available on supported Ricoh models, and updates still need someone responsible for approving and installing them.

## What Is Ricoh Always Current Technology?

Ricoh Always Current Technology is a feature built into Ricoh's newer multifunction printers (machines that print, copy, scan, and often fax) that lets the device's software be updated and expanded after you buy it. Ricoh describes it as a way to "enjoy the benefits of the latest functionalities, technologies, and features, regardless of when you purchase the device" ([Ricoh Latin America](https://www.ricoh-americalatina.com/en/products/always-current-technology-instructions-and-versions)).

Ricoh launched the technology in early 2019 alongside its IM range of "Intelligent Devices." In Australia, for example, it arrived on six A3 colour models: the IM C2000, IM C2500, IM C3000, IM C3500, IM C4500, and IM C6000 ([Ricoh Australia](https://www.ricoh.com.au/news/ricoh-unveils-new-intelligent-devices)). Newer Ricoh IM models have continued to include it as a standard feature.

Why does a printer need updates at all? Because a modern office printer is really a networked computer, with its own operating system, storage, and touchscreen. Like any computer, its software ages and develops weaknesses. Always Current Technology is Ricoh's answer to that.

## How Does It Work in Practice?

Always Current Technology works much like the update system on a smartphone. Ricoh describes it as a standard feature that automatically downloads updates and then prompts you to activate them at a convenient time ([Ricoh Latin America](https://www.ricoh-americalatina.com/en/products/always-current-technology-instructions-and-versions)).

In simple terms, the process looks like this:

1. **Ricoh releases an update.** Updates are grouped into numbered versions (for example V1.1, V1.2, and so on). Each version bundles improvements such as new screens, new features, or security changes.
2. **The printer becomes aware of it.** Because the device is connected to your network and the internet, it can find and download the new software.
3. **Someone approves the installation.** An administrator, or your Ricoh service provider, chooses when to install the update. This is usually done outside busy hours so staff are not interrupted.
4. **The new features appear on the touchscreen.** Once installed, the new options show up on the device's Smart Operation Panel, the tablet-style touchscreen on the front of Ricoh's intelligent devices.

There is a second part to the system: apps. Ricoh runs an online store called the RICOH Application Site, where new applications can be downloaded directly to supported devices ([Ricoh Latin America](https://www.ricoh-americalatina.com/en/products/always-current-technology)). These can add specific capabilities, such as connecting the printer to a cloud storage service, without replacing any hardware.

The key point for a business owner is this: according to Ricoh, there is no need to call a technician or wait for a replacement machine to get new functions ([Ricoh Europe](https://www.ricoh-europe.com/products/always-current-technology/)).

## What Kind of Updates Does It Deliver?

Always Current Technology delivers three main types of updates: usability improvements, new features, and security enhancements. Here are real examples from Ricoh's published version notes, so you can see what this means in practice.

### Usability and new features

- **Version 1.1** introduced a new layout for the Settings screen, new features in the Smart Copy app, and a new option to choose file types and save locations in the Fax settings ([Ricoh Asia Pacific](https://www.ricoh-ap.com/dynamic-workplace-intelligence/ricoh-always-current-technology/ract-learn-more/Updates)).
- **Version 1.2** added interface improvements to the Smart Operation Panel, new features in Smart Copy and Smart Fax, enhanced IT management features, and general usability improvements ([Ricoh Asia Pacific](https://www.ricoh-ap.com/dynamic-workplace-intelligence/ricoh-always-current-technology/ract-learn-more/Updates)).

None of these changes required a new machine. They arrived as software on printers already in offices.

### Security improvements

Security is where the technology earns its keep. Ricoh's published security updates include ([Ricoh Thailand](https://www.ricoh.co.th/en/dynamic-workplace-intelligence/ricoh-always-current-technology/learn-more/security-update)):

- **Hidden password entry.** Users can choose to hide characters as they type a password on the control panel, so a colleague standing nearby cannot read it.
- **Blocking risky file types.** Administrators can stop the device from printing PostScript and PDF files, which are printer languages that can run scripts. Ricoh notes this function complies with NIST 800-171, a United States government standard for protecting sensitive information.
- **Safer guest printing.** Guest users can print wirelessly directly to the device even when access controls are switched on, so visitors can print without being given access to the wider network.

On a printer without an update path, a weakness found after purchase stays there for the rest of the machine's life.

## Why Does It Matter for Your Business?

Always Current Technology matters because it turns your printer from a fixed purchase into a device that keeps pace with your business. There are four practical benefits.

### 1. Stronger protection against data breaches

Printers are a real and often overlooked security risk. Quocirca's Print Security Landscape 2025, a survey of 400 IT decision makers in the United States and Europe published in July 2025, found that 56 percent of organisations reported at least one print-related data loss in the previous year ([Quocirca](https://quocirca.com/content/quocirca-print-security-landscape-2025-press-release/)). The same study put the average cost of a print-related breach at £630,000 for businesses with a single-brand printer fleet, rising to £937,000 for those running a mix of brands.

A printer that receives regular security updates is a much harder target than one frozen at its original software. For businesses handling client records, contracts, or personal data, that alone can justify the choice.

### 2. A longer, more useful life for the machine

Most businesses keep an office printer for several years. Without updates, a device is at its most modern on installation day and dates steadily after that. With Always Current Technology, it can gain features that did not exist when it was bought, so it stays useful for years, not just months.

### 3. Less disruption and fewer service visits

Ricoh says the updates are designed to minimise downtime ([Ricoh Latin America](https://www.ricoh-americalatina.com/en/products/always-current-technology-instructions-and-versions)). Because new features arrive as downloads, there is no need to book an engineer to fit hardware or wait for a machine swap. Updates can be scheduled for evenings or weekends, so staff keep working as normal.

### 4. More value from the same investment

When you invest in a printer, you pay for what it can do. If that grows over time with no extra hardware, you get more from the same spend, and you never need to replace a device just to gain a new feature such as scanning to a cloud service.

## What Are the Limitations?

Always Current Technology is useful, but it has limits worth knowing before you rely on it.

- **It only works on supported models.** It is built into Ricoh's newer intelligent devices, mainly the IM series. Older Ricoh machines and other brands do not use it.
- **It needs a network connection.** The device must be able to reach Ricoh's update service to download anything.
- **Someone must take responsibility.** Your IT team or service provider should approve and schedule updates, then check the security settings afterwards.
- **Software cannot change the hardware.** An update cannot make a slow printer faster or turn a black-and-white machine into a colour one. Choose the right hardware for your volume first.
- **Some apps may cost extra.** Specific applications or connected services may be priced separately, so check with your supplier.

## How to Get the Most From It

1. **Confirm your model supports it.** The model name on the device usually starts with "IM." Ask your supplier to confirm the feature is enabled.
2. **Agree who owns updates.** Decide whether your IT team or your service provider approves and installs them, and write it into your service agreement.
3. **Set a schedule.** Review available updates every quarter, and install security updates as soon as practical.
4. **Explore the application site.** Ask your supplier which apps on the RICOH Application Site could save your team time.

## The Bottom Line

Ricoh Always Current Technology brings the smartphone model of regular updates to the office printer. The result is a device that stays more secure, gains features over time, and holds its value longer, without extra hardware or engineer visits. It only works on supported Ricoh models, and someone must own the updates. But for any business that handles sensitive documents, a printer that keeps improving after installation is a simple way to reduce risk and get more from the investment.
